# Israeli Bagrut Physics & Pre-University (מכינה) Physics — Research Report

**Purpose:** Calibrate lesson depth, build dependency trees, design mock exams, and identify stable vs. variable exam patterns.  
**Scope:** Bagrut Physics 5 units (שאלון 036 series), 2015–2024; pre-university physics (מכינה / classification exams, including course 7001 contexts).  
**Sources:** Ministry of Education pedagogical portal, official exam PDFs (2021–2024), Ministry insights document (תשפ"ג), Technion/BIU pre-academic programs, PPST research (Shakhman & Barak, 2019), Neaman Institute longitudinal analysis (1975–2014).  
**Date:** June 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Israeli **Bagrut Physics at 5 units** is not a single exam but **four separate external questionnaires** plus **30% school-based assessment** (הערכה חלופית). The stable core since the **תשע"ה curriculum reform** is:

| Questionnaire | Code (current) | Weight | Duration | Choice rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| מכניקה (Mechanics) | **036-361** | 30% | 2 hours | Answer **3 of 6** |
| חשמל (Electricity & Magnetism) | **036-371** | 25% | 2 hours | Answer **3 of 6** |
| קרינה וחומר (Radiation & Matter) | **036-282** | 30% | 2 hours | Answer **3 of 5** |
| שאלון חקר / מעבדה (Research/Lab) | **036-382** / **036-386** | 15% | 2–2.5 hours | Structured (not free choice) |

**Pre-university מכינה** (Technion classification, BIU summer mechina, etc.) covers **mechanics + E&M only**, excludes modern physics/optics (Technion since 2019), runs **longer and more derivation-heavy** than Bagrut, and gates university Physics 1 (114077 / פיזיקה 1ל') and Physics 2 (114078 / פיזיקה 2ל').

## 1. Bagrut Physics Exam Structure

### 1.1 Overall architecture (5 יח"ל)

Since **תשע"ה (2014–15)**, all 5-unit students follow the **"meaningful learning" curriculum** (למידה משמעותית), covering mechanics, electricity & magnetism, radiation & matter, plus mandatory lab/research work.

**Final grade composition:**
- **30%** — School assessment (הערכה חלופית): projects, internal tests, lab work across grades 10–12. Can be retaken via external exam.
- **70%** — External written questionnaires (weighted as above).

**Internal school exams** (not the final Bagrut, but shape preparation):
- **Grade 11 — שאלון 35481 (804):** ~3.5 hours, **5 of 8** questions, 20 pts each (65% of internal grade).
- **Grade 12 — שאלון 35482 (805):** ~1 hr 45 min, **3 of 5** questions, 33.33 pts each (35% of internal grade).

**Legacy code mapping** (pre-תשע"ט unified to current codes):
- 36002 → 36371 (electricity)
- 36201 → 36361 (mechanics)
- 36282 → 36282 (radiation — unchanged)
- 36382 → 36382 (research — unchanged)

**3-unit shortcut:** Students who take only mechanics + electricity (and skip radiation/lab) can bundle to **036-300** for 3 יח"ל credit.

### 1.2 Point allocation and choice mechanics

Within each written questionnaire:
- Each answered question = **33.33 points** (100 ÷ 3).
- Sub-items (א, ב, ג…) carry explicit point values (typically 4–10 pts each).
- **Critical rule:** If a student answers more than the required number, **only the first N answers are graded** (N = 3 for mechanics/electricity/radiation).
- Students must **clearly mark question and sub-item numbers**.

**What is NOT optional:**
- All four questionnaire types are required for full 5-unit certification (unless using פיזיקה בגישה חוקרת as an integrated alternative track).
- Within each questionnaire, **topic choice is entirely student-driven** — there is no mandated pairing (e.g., you may skip gravitation entirely if you answer kinematics, dynamics, projectile, energy, momentum questions instead).

### 1.3 Schedule and administration (typical summer moed)

Exams run on **separate days** (not one continuous block):

| Day | Typical order (2024–2025) | Form | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Electricity | 36371 | 10:00–12:00 |
| Day 1 | Mechanics | 36361 | 12:30–14:30 |
| Day 1 | Radiation & Matter | 36282 | 15:00–17:00 |
| Day 2 | Research/Lab | 36382 / 36386 | 10:00–12:00 / 12:30 |

Three moedim per year: **winter (January), summer (May–July), autumn (September–October)**.

### 1.4 Formula sheet, calculator, and materials

From official exam covers (2022–2024):

| Item | Policy |
|---|---|
| **Calculator** | **Non-graphing scientific calculator only.** No programmable features. |
| **Formula sheet** | **Attached נספח** — constants, kinematic equations, Coulomb, Ohm, lens, wave, atomic, nuclear formulas. |
| **g** | Use **g = 10 m/s²** (explicitly stated). |
| **Writing** | Answers in **pen**; pencil only for diagrams/graphs. |
| **Graphs** | Draw straight lines with **ruler**; radiation exam requires **≥ half page** per graph. |
| **Draft paper** | Only in official exam booklet; extra draft pages risk disqualification. |

> Note: Some third-party prep sites mention graphing calculators; **official 2022–2024 booklets specify non-graphing.** Curriculum materials should follow the official rule.

### 1.5 Required answer process (what must appear on paper)

Official instructions require, for computational questions:

1. **Write the formula** as given on the formula sheet (symbolic form).
2. **Algebraic manipulation** — including changing subject of formula when needed.
3. **Explicit substitution** of numerical values before computing.
4. **Final answer** with appropriate **significant figures** and **units**.
5. **Qualitative justification** when asked (נמקו, הסבירו) — not just numbers.

The Ministry's **תשפ"ג insights document** emphasizes that skipping step 3 (substitution) is a widespread failure mode and may raise suspicion of copying. Examiners expect:

- **Free-body diagrams** with labeled forces, axes, and identifying agent when requested.
- **Conservation-law justification** — students must state *why* energy/momentum is conserved in the given situation.
- **Graph literacy** — reading v–t, I–V, Ek–x graphs; plotting scatter + trend line; slope from grid intersections.
- **Parametric expressions** before plugging numbers (especially in first sub-items worth 7–9 points).

## 2. Topic Coverage (Bagrut Physics, 2015–2024)

### 2.1 Structural stability vs. content variability

**Stable (2015–2024):**
- Four-questionnaire architecture and 3-of-6 / 3-of-5 choice format.
- Broad topic buckets per question slot (see tables below).
- Multi-sub-item questions with mixed computation + explanation.
- At least one **multi-topic synthesis question** per mechanics and electricity exam.
- Research exam: **75% unseen experiment + 25% mandatory lab experiments** from coursework.

**Variable:**
- Specific story contexts (e.g., Olympic gymnastics 2022, Beresheet spacecraft gravitation).
- Which sub-topic gets the synthesis/combined question (e.g., momentum+energy vs. E-field+B-field).
- Annual **מיקוד** (focus topics) published by מפמ"ר — certain sub-topics may be emphasized or de-emphasized in a given year.
- Internal grade 11/12 exam proportions.

### 2.2 Mechanics (036-361) — topic frequency

Analysis of Ministry mapping (תשפ"ג) plus exams 2018–2024:

| Topic | Appears as dedicated Q? | Frequency | Typical skills tested |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Kinematics (1D/2D graphs)** | Q1 almost always | **~95%+** | Read v–t graph; classify motion phases; avg velocity; braking distance |
| **Newton's laws / Atwood / incline** | Q2 | **~90%+** | FBD; ΣF=ma; tension; friction on slope; multi-part lab-style setups |
| **Projectile / 2D motion** | Q3 | **~85%+** | Horizontal launch from moving body; energy shortcuts; trajectory reasoning |
| **Energy / vertical circular motion** | Q4 | **~85%+** | Parametric normal force at top; Ek graph; friction loops |
| **Momentum + energy combined** | Q5 | **~80%+** | Inelastic/elastic collisions; friction incline + horizontal; conservation reasoning |
| **Gravitation / orbits** | Q6 | **~75%+** | Universal gravitation; Kepler; satellite energy; qualitative orbit diagrams |
| **Simple harmonic motion** | Sometimes within Q4/Q6 | **~40%** | Pendulum period (more common in lab exam than written mechanics) |
| **Ideal gas** | Curriculum topic | **Rare in written exam** | Listed in curriculum; infrequent as standalone Bagrut question 2015–2024 |
| **Circular motion (horizontal)** | Embedded | **~60%** | Conical pendulum, rotating table systems |

**Consistently tested sub-skills across mechanics:**
- Graph → physics inference (not just area-under-curve).
- **Explicit FBD** in ~2–3 of 6 questions per exam.
- **Parametric derivation** before numeric answer (weighted heavily in scoring).
- **Order-of-magnitude sanity check** (examiners penalize billion-km answers for cm-scale setups).

### 2.3 Electricity & Magnetism (036-371)

| Topic | Dedicated Q slot | Frequency | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Electrostatics (Coulomb, shells, potential)** | Q1 | **~90%+** | Charged spherical shell; equipotentials; energy in field |
| **DC circuits (Ohm, Kirchhoff-style)** | Q2–Q3 | **~95%+** | Variable resistor; internal resistance; voltmeter placement; efficiency |
| **Capacitors** | Curriculum + embedded | **~50%** | More often in school assessment than standalone Bagrut Q |
| **Magnetic fields / forces on wires** | Q4 | **~85%+** | Tangent galvanometer; linearize graph (sin θ vs B) |
| **Particle motion in E and/or B** | Q5 | **~80%+** | Velocity selector; separate E-only and B-only segments |
| **Electromagnetic induction (Faraday/Lenz)** | Q6 | **~75%+** | Induced EMF via flux; derive I from dΦ/dt |

**Electricity-specific patterns:**
- **Graph reading** (I vs. position of slider) as central skill — students must use **marked points on provided graph**, not table points.
- **Qualitative circuit reasoning** (what happens to bulb 2 when bulb 1 is full brightness?) — heavily discriminates top scores.
- Combined Q5 (E+B) tests **independent understanding** of each field separately before synthesis.

### 2.4 Waves / Optics / Modern Physics — Radiation & Matter (036-282)

| Topic | 2023 exam Q | Frequency 2015–2024 | Depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mechanical waves (1D)** | Q1 | **~70%** | Pulse propagation; boundary reflection; v = √(T/μ) |
| **Physical optics (interference/diffraction)** | Q2 | **~80%** | Double slit; diffraction grating; white-light spectra |
| **Geometric optics (lenses/mirrors)** | Rotates | **~60%** | Lens formula; ray diagrams; less dominant than physical optics recently |
| **Photoelectric effect** | Q3 | **~75%** | I–V curves; work function; saturation current |
| **Atomic structure / Bohr / spectra** | Q4 | **~80%** | Absorption/emission; energy level diagrams; ionization |
| **Nuclear / radioactivity** | Q5 | **~70%** | Half-life; decay constant; dating reasoning (qualitative) |
| **EM waves (general)** | Curriculum | **~50%** | Often integrated with optics rather than standalone |

**Modern physics depth:** Conceptual and semi-quantitative — **no relativity calculations, no Schrödinger**. Students draw energy diagrams, compute λ from ΔE, use E=hf, but at **algebra level**, not calculus.

### 2.5 Research / Lab questionnaire (036-382)

**Structure (stable since ~2021):**
- **Part A (75%):** Unseen experiment — recent example: pendulum period vs. length/mass/configuration.
- **Part B (25%):** Questions on **mandatory experiments** from mechanics + electricity coursework (student picks one mechanics experiment; one fixed electricity experiment — e.g., EMF and terminal voltage).

**Skills weighted:**
- Measurement technique & **period counting** (full cycles, not half).
- **Relative uncertainty** and comparing two experimental methods.
- Scatter plots, trend lines, slope units.
- Describing **what was measured vs. what is plotted** (common failure).
- System diagram from memory (correct components per approved lab manual).

## 3. Pre-University Physics (מכינה)

### 3.1 What "מכינה" means in the Israeli ecosystem

The term covers **several distinct programs**:

| Program | Audience | Content | Exit requirement |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Technion classification (בחינת סיווג)** | Admitted Technion students lacking physics prereqs | Mechanics → then Electricity | Pass each part (or Bagrut ≥70 in 4+ units, or pass 114077/114078) |
| **Technion pre-academic center courses** | Same | Mirrors classification syllabus | Prepare for classification exam |
| **BIU summer mechina** | HS students entering physics/engineering tracks | Mechanics + E&M | ≥75 per part, ≥80 average |
| **BIU online HS mechina** | Pre-enrollment HS students | Mechanics only (10 topics) | Completion certificate |
| **University mechina (TAU etc.)** | Conditional acceptance | Full 5-unit Bagrut-equivalent in physics + math + English | Bagrut-level exams |

**Course 7001** appears in BIU/Technion contexts as the **mechanics module identifier** in pre-academic physics sequences (paired with electricity module); it is **not** a Ministry Bagrut form number.

### 3.2 Scope vs. Bagrut 5 units

| Dimension | Bagrut 5u | מכינה / Classification |
|---|---|---|
| **Topics** | Mechanics + E&M + Radiation/Matter + Lab | **Mechanics + E&M only** |
| **Modern physics / optics** | ~30% of external grade | **Excluded** (Technion explicitly since 2019) |
| **Lab / research** | 15% dedicated exam | Minimal or absent in classification |
| **Duration per exam** | 2 hours | **2.5 hours** per part (Technion) |
| **Calculator** | Non-graphing | **Scientific calculator** |
| **Draft paper** | Booklet only | **No separate draft** — all in booklet |
| **Derivations** | Parametric + numeric | **More open-ended derivations**, university-style |
| **Pass threshold** | Scaled 0–100 Bagrut grade | Pass/fail (Technion); BIU mechina 75/80 rule |

### 3.3 Bridge to university Physics 1 & 2

**Technion pathway:**
- Classification **Mechanics (part A)** → unlocks **Physics 1 / 114077 (פיזיקה 1ל')**
- Classification **Electricity (part B)** → unlocks **Physics 2 / 114078 (פיזיקה 2ל')**
- Deadlines vary by faculty: some require mechanics before **semester 2**, electricity before **semester 3–4**.
- **Exemptions:** Bagrut physics ≥4 units with grade ≥70; engineering-technician track exams ≥70 in technical mechanics/electricity.

**BIU mechina syllabus (mechanics)** — canonical pre-uni list:
1. Models, measurement, vectors  
2. 1D motion & acceleration  
3. 2D motion & **projectiles**  
4. Newton's laws  
5. Applications of Newton's laws  
6. Universal gravitation  
7. Circular motion  
8. Work & energy  
9. Impulse & momentum  
10. Simple harmonic motion  

Electricity module adds: Coulomb, fields, Gauss (qualitative), potential, DC circuits, capacitors, magnetic fields, induction — aligned with **first half of university E&M**, not Bagrut radiation topics.

### 3.4 What's unique to מכינה (not in HS or university intro)

- **Compressed timeline** (4–8 weeks) with high problem volume.
- **Explicit vector-component algebra** before physics concepts (more systematic than typical HS pacing).
- **No formula-sheet safety net** on classification exams — students must know core relations.
- **Failure on process:** classification exams often require **full derivation chains** similar to university homework, not Bagrut-style "first sub-item worth 9 pts for parametric expression."
- **No choice architecture** — typically comprehensive section-based exams, not "3 of 6."

### 3.5 Question style comparison

| Style element | Bagrut | מכינה / Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Computation | High | **Very high** |
| Symbolic derivation | Required in parts | **Central throughout** |
| Graph sketching | Moderate | Moderate |
| Qualitative "explain why" | High (discriminates grades) | Moderate |
| Multi-step story problems | Yes (Hebrew narrative) | Shorter, math-forward |
| Lab / experimental | 15% separate exam | Rare |

## 4. Question Type Analysis for Physics

### 4.1 Free-body diagrams (FBD)

**Estimated fraction:** **~35–45% of answered mechanics questions** require drawing or interpreting FBDs (typically 1–2 of the 3 chosen questions, almost always present in the dynamics and energy questions available).

In electricity, **force diagrams on charges/wires** appear in ~15–20% of chosen sets (electrostatics + magnetic force questions).

### 4.2 Energy conservation vs. Newton's laws

Both appear in **every mechanics exam**, but roles differ:

| Approach | Role | PPST level (typical) |
|---|---|---|
| **Kinematics graphs** | Q1 entry | Diagnosis |
| **ΣF = ma** | Q2 core | Diagnosis–Strategy |
| **Energy methods** | Q4–Q5 shortcut or required | Strategy |
| **Momentum** | Q5 collisions | Strategy–Conceptual |

**~25–35%** of chosen mechanics questions can be solved with energy **instead of** force methods, but exams **force explicit method justification** — using energy without explaining why conservation applies loses credit.

**Multi-concept (e.g., projectile + energy):** At least **1 combined question per exam** (~17% of exam weight if chosen); Ministry notes Q5 (momentum+energy) and Q3 (projectile+energy) as recurring synthesis slots.

### 4.3 Answer form distribution (mechanics + electricity written exams)

Estimated from תשפ"ג item tagging and PPST analysis:

| Answer form | Share of sub-items |
|---|---|
| Numerical + units | ~40% |
| Qualitative explanation / justification | ~25% |
| Parametric expression development | ~20% |
| Graph plotting / reading | ~10% |
| Diagram drawing (FBD, circuit, ray) | ~5% |

### 4.4 Research exam question types

| Type | Part A (unseen) | Part B (known labs) |
|---|---|---|
| Direct measurement | ~40% | ~20% |
| Graph + slope | ~25% | ~15% |
| Uncertainty / relative difference | ~20% | ~10% |
| System description from memory | — | ~30% |
| Theory prediction vs. data | ~15% | ~25% |

## 5. Difficulty Calibration

### 5.1 Math prerequisites (Bagrut)

**Required (4–5 unit math assumed):**
- Algebra: rearranging formulas, simultaneous equations, quadratic equations.
- Trigonometry: sin/cos/tan on inclines, circular motion components.
- Vectors: decomposition, adding perpendicular components; **not** dot/cross product formalism.
- Graphs: linear slope, piecewise interpretation, area under v–t curve.
- Proportional reasoning, scientific notation, significant figures.

**NOT required on Bagrut:**
- Calculus (derivatives/integrals) — **explicitly absent** from 5u physics curriculum.
- Differential equations.
- Matrix algebra (linear algebra appears in university, not Bagrut).

**Common math gaps that hurt physics scores:**
- Unit conversion (cm↔m, mm ruler misread).
- Confusing **graph of velocity vs. graph of position**.
- Linearization (plotting sin θ vs. B to get straight line).
- Sign conventions in 1D collision problems.

### 5.2 Difficulty range within a single exam

Using **PPST taxonomy** applied to national exams (Shakhman & Barak, n=18,000):

| PPST level | Mechanics weight | Electricity weight | Mean student score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Retrieval | 4% | 4% | **92%** |
| Diagnosis | 29% | 48% | **84%** |
| Strategy | 21% | 24% | **64%** |
| Conceptual | 33% | 20% | **58%** |
| Creative | 13% | 4% | (rare items) |

**Within one exam, difficulty span:**
- **Easiest sub-items (~85% pass):** define photoelectric effect; read amplitude from graph; identify correct formula.
- **Hardest sub-items (~35% pass):** linearization for galvanometer; prove non-dependence in pendulum theory; combined friction+collision energy accounting.

**Neaman Institute (1975–2014) historical note:** Exams became **more structured and accessible** over decades (more choice, clearer rubrics), yet **conceptual demand remained high** — national enrollment in 5u physics did **not** increase significantly despite easier formal structure.

### 5.3 What separates ~90 from ~60?

| Score band | Profile |
|---|---|
| **~90+** | Chooses wisely (plays to strengths); complete **parametric → substitute → units** chain; correct FBDs; justifies conservation laws; reads graphs using marked points; order-of-magnitude checks. |
| **~70–80** | Strong on diagnosis-level routine problems; loses points on strategy/conceptual sub-items; incomplete explanations. |
| **~60–65** | Can solve familiar templates; weak on combined topics; poor graph skills; skips justification; unit/sign errors. |
| **<55** | Fails conceptual items (~58% class average); lab exam weak on mandatory experiment recall. |

**Discriminating sub-skills (highest PPST correlation, ρ ≈ −0.70):**
1. Justifying **when** conservation laws apply.
2. **Circuit qualitative reasoning** (not just formula plug-in).
3. **Graph linearization** and slope with correct units.
4. **Multi-stage problems** where early error propagates (examiners award partial credit only with visible correct method).

## 6. Dependency Tree

### 6.1 Mechanics chain

```
Math foundations
├── Algebra & units
├── Trigonometry
└── Vectors (components)
    │
    ▼
Kinematics (1D: v, a, graphs)
├── Kinematics (2D: independence of x/y)
│   └── Projectile motion
│       └── Relative motion (rare advanced)
├── Newton's 1st (inertia) ← qualitative
├── Newton's 2nd (ΣF=ma)
│   ├── Inclined plane
│   ├── Atwood / pulleys
│   ├── Friction (static/kinetic)
│   └── Circular motion (horizontal & vertical)
│       └── Vertical loop / normal force at top
├── Newton's 3rd ← action-reaction pairs (exam: sensors, blocks)
├── Work & kinetic energy
│   ├── Work by variable force (graph area)
│   ├── Potential energy (gravity, elastic)
│   └── Conservation of mechanical energy
│       └── Used to shortcut dynamics problems
├── Momentum & impulse
│   ├── Elastic / inelastic collisions
│   └── Combined with energy (Q5 archetype)
├── Gravitation
│   ├── Universal law
│   ├── Orbits & Kepler's 3rd
│   └── Escape / orbital energy (qualitative)
└── Simple harmonic motion
    └── Pendulum period (links to lab exam)
```

**Math prerequisites flagged as commonly weak:**
- **Vector decomposition** → blocks projectile + inclined plane.
- **Reading v–t vs. x–t** → blocks all kinematics graph questions.
- **tan/sin/cos on non-right triangles** → blocks gravitation angle problems.

### 6.2 Electricity & Magnetism chain

```
Math foundations
├── Algebra
└── Basic graph reading
    │
    ▼
Electrostatics
├── Coulomb's law
├── Electric field (point, uniform, shell)
├── Potential & potential energy
└── Capacitance (parallel plate, Q=CV)
    │
    ▼
DC Circuits
├── Current, voltage, resistance
├── Ohm's law
├── Series / parallel
├── EMF & internal resistance
├── Power & efficiency
└── Kirchhoff-style multi-loop (qualitative + numeric)
    │
    ▼
Magnetism
├── Magnetic force on moving charge (F=qvB)
├── Force on current-carrying wire (F=ILB)
├── Right-hand rule
└── Motion in uniform B (circular path)
    │
    ▼
Electromagnetic induction
├── Magnetic flux
├── Faraday's law (magnitude)
├── Lenz's law (direction)
└── Induced current in circuits
```

**Cross-links to mechanics:** Energy/work analogies; particle dynamics in fields mirrors projectile structure.

### 6.4 Pre-university (מכינה) → University bridge

```
מכינה mechanics (7001-level)
└── Physics 1 / 114077
    ├── Calculus-based kinematics
    ├── Rotation (extended beyond Bagrut)
    └── Oscillations & waves (mechanical)

מכינה electricity
└── Physics 2 / 114078
    ├── Gauss's law (full)
    ├── Capacitors & dielectrics (quantitative)
```

## 7. Common Student Errors and Teaching Gaps

### 7.1 Ministry-documented errors (תשפ"ג national insights)

**Cross-cutting (all written exams):**
| Error | Frequency | Teaching gap |
|---|---|---|
| No explicit substitution in formulas | Very high | Teachers accept jump to final number in homework |
| Missing units / wrong sig figs | High | Insufficient enforcement on every problem |
| Disorganized answers / illegible scan | Moderate | Process not practiced under exam conditions |
| Graph scale not uniform | Moderate | Graph taught once, not spiraled |
| Using table points instead of trend line for slope | Moderate | Over-emphasis on "points from table" rule |

**Mechanics-specific:**
| Error | Topic |
|---|---|
| Misreading v–t graph phases | Kinematics Q1 |
| FBD missing axes or force agents | Dynamics Q2 |
| Treating horizontal & vertical motion incorrectly in projectiles | Q3 |
| Confusing velocity zero at top with zero normal force / detachment | Circular motion Q4 |
| Applying conservation without checking external work (friction) | Q5 — **most widespread content error** |
| Confusing g at surface vs. orbit altitude | Gravitation Q6 |

**Electricity-specific:**
| Error | Topic |
|---|---|
| Orders-of-magnitude errors in field/potential | Q1 |
| Thinking voltmeter in series doesn't affect circuit | Q2 |
| Changing R_var affects current but **not** terminal voltage reasoning | Q2–Q3 — **very widespread** |
| Ignoring equal bulb currents when stated | Q3 |
| Wrong linearization variable for galvanometer graph | Q4 |
| Particle moves in E field → speed must change (false generalization) | Q5 |

**Lab / research exam:**
| Error | Topic |
|---|---|
| Measuring half-period instead of full period | Pendulum unseen |
| Measuring 10 periods then re-measuring 1 period separately | Uncertainty reduction not understood |
| Confusing measured quantity with graph axis (L vs. 1/L) | Graph variables |
| Cannot describe mandatory experiment setup from memory | Part B — **indicates lab taught as cookbook** |
| Confusing open vs. short in EMF experiment | Electricity lab Q12 |

### 7.2 Teachers emphasize vs. exam actually tests

| Teachers often emphasize | Exam actually rewards |
|---|---|
| Memorizing formula sheet layouts | **Selecting method** (energy vs. Newton) with justification |
| Repetitive numeric drills | **Parametric derivation** then numeric |
| Cookbook lab reports | **Unseen experiment design** + uncertainty reasoning |
| Topic silos (finish mechanics unit, move on) | **Synthesis questions** crossing topics |
| Template problem types | **Novel contexts** (road safety, spacecraft, gymnastics) |
| "Error calculation formula" by rote | **Relative difference between two experimental methods** |
| Geometric optics ray rules | **Physical optics** (interference/grating) — increasing share in radiation exam |

### 7.3 Implications for curriculum / mock exam design

1. **Mock exams should mirror choice architecture:** 6Q choose 3 (mechanics/electricity), 5Q choose 3 (radiation), with **33.33 pts per question** and multi-part rubrics.
2. **Every mock mechanics exam should include:** one graph-heavy kinematics Q, one FBD+dynamics Q, one synthesis (momentum/energy or projectile+energy) Q.
3. **Every mock electricity exam should include:** one electrostatics Q, one DC circuit with qualitative reasoning, one induction or particle-in-field Q.
4. **Process marks:** Reserve 20–30% of points for **method visible** (formula → algebra → substitute → units).
5. **Lab component:** Include one unseen-style measurement sequence + one "describe mandatory experiment" prompt.
6. **Pre-uni track:** Strip radiation/modern; add ** longer derivations**, no formula sheet, 2.5-hour timing, calculator-only policy.
7. **Difficulty mix (PPST-target):** ~45% diagnosis, ~25% strategy, ~20% conceptual, ~10% retrieval for Bagrut-faithful mocks.
