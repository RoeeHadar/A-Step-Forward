# Electric Fields & Gauss's Law

**HE:** שדות חשמליים וחוק גאוס

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Gauss's Law for the Electric Field
**HE:** חוק גאוס לשדה החשמלי

Coulomb's law can compute the field of any charge distribution — but only if you integrate over every charge element.

> חוק קולון יכול לחשב את השדה של כל פילוג מטען — אבל רק אם מאחדים על כל אלמנט מטען.

## Skill atoms

- Coulomb's law and superposition for point charges
- Electric field E = F/q as a vector field
- Field of dipole and continuous charge distributions
- Gauss's law ∮ E·dA = Q_enc/ε₀
- Symmetry choice for Gaussian surfaces (sphere, cylinder, plane)
- Field of infinite line/plane/shell from Gauss's law
- Flux through closed surfaces

## Level scope

- **physics1:** Electrostatics at university depth — Coulomb superposition, field lines, and Gauss's law with symmetry arguments on finals.
