## Skill atoms

- Kinetic energy KE = ½mv²
- Gravitational PE = mgh
- Elastic PE = ½kx²
- Conservation: KE_i + PE_i = KE_f + PE_f (no friction)
- Energy bar diagrams
- Roller coaster / pendulum problems
- Finding speed at a position from height
