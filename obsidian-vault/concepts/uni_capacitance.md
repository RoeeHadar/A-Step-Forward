## Skill atoms

- Capacitance C = Q/V definition
- Parallel-plate capacitor C = ε₀A/d
- Energy stored U = ½CV² = ½Q²/C
- Series and parallel capacitor combinations
- Dielectric constant κ and field reduction in matter
- Gauss's law with dielectrics (bound charges)
