# Projectile Motion

**HE:** זריקה

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Projectile Motion
**HE:** תנועת קליע

Horizontal and angled projectile motion. Independent x/y components, time of flight, range, maximum height, and velocity at any point.

> תנועת קליע אופקית וזוויתית. רכיבי x/y עצמאיים, זמן טיסה, טווח, גובה מקסימלי ומהירות בכל נקודה.

## Skill atoms

- Horizontal throw (zero initial vertical velocity)
- Angled throw (general projectile)
- Decomposing initial velocity into x and y components
- Time of flight
- Range (horizontal distance)
- Maximum height
- Velocity at any time (components and magnitude)
- Launch from elevated position

## Level scope

- **hs_physics:** A staple Bagrut mechanics question type — horizontal or angled launch with 3–4 sub-parts (range, max height, flight time, impact velocity). g = 10 m/s² is standard; answers must include units and clear decomposition.
