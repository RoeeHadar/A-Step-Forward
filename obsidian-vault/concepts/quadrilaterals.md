# Quadrilaterals

**HE:** מרובעים

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Quadrilaterals — Properties, Proofs, and Area Formulas
**HE:** מרובעים — תכונות, הוכחות ונוסחאות שטח

Quadrilaterals — properties, proofs, and area formulas for parallelograms, rhombus, rectangle, square, and trapezoid, including the midline theorem and five sufficient conditions for parallelogram proofs.
