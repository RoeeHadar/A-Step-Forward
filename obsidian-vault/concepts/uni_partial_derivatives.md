## Skill atoms

- Compute ∂f/∂x and ∂f/∂y by treating other variables as constants
- Second-order partials f_xx, f_xy, f_yx, f_yy
- Clairaut: f_xy=f_yx when continuous
- Directional derivative D_u f = ∇f·u
- Gradient orthogonal to level curves
- Local max/min via critical points where ∇f=0
- Second derivative test in two variables (discriminant D)
- Lagrange multipliers for constrained extrema

## Level scope

- **calculus_2:** Partial derivatives through optimization; gradient, directional derivatives, and Lagrange multipliers are core calc-2 exam topics.
