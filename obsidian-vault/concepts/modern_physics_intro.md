## Skill atoms

- Photoelectric effect: light ejects electrons from metal
- Photon energy: E = hf = hc/λ
- Work function: minimum energy to eject electron
- Einstein's photoelectric equation: KE_max = hf - φ
- Threshold frequency
- Wave-particle duality
- Planck's constant h
- de Broglie wavelength: λ = h/p

## Level scope

- **hs_physics:** Photoelectric effect and photon energy E = hf — anchor topic of the radiation questionnaire. Exam items use Einstein's equation KE_max = hf − φ, threshold frequency, and qualitative wave-particle duality; de Broglie may appear as a short calculation.
