## Skill atoms

- Lorentz force F = qv×B and right-hand rule
- Force on current-carrying wire I L×B
- Circular motion of charge in uniform B (r = mv/qB)
- Biot–Savart / Ampère's law for symmetric geometries
- Field of long straight wire and solenoid
- Torque on magnetic dipole τ = μ×B

## Level scope

- **physics1:** Magnetism chapter — Lorentz force, wire forces, and symmetric B-field calculations using Ampère/Biot–Savart patterns.
