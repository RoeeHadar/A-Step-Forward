## Skill atoms

- Torque: τ = r·F·sinθ
- Conditions for static equilibrium: ΣF=0 and Στ=0
- Center of gravity
- Balancing a beam on a pivot
- Ladder against wall problems
- Uniform beam supported at two points

## Level scope

- **hs_physics:** Static equilibrium with torques — ladder-on-wall and beam-on-pivot problems are classic Bagrut mechanics items. Both ΣF = 0 and Στ = 0 must be applied; center of gravity location is often a sub-part.
