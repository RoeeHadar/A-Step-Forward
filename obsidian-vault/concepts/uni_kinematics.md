# Advanced Kinematics

**HE:** קינמטיקה מתקדמת

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Kinematics in One Dimension
**HE:** קינמטיקה בממד אחד

Kinematics describes motion without considering forces. In 1D we work with displacement, velocity, and acceleration along a single axis, using four kinematic equations for constant acceleration.

> קינמטיקה מתארת תנועה ללא התייחסות לכוחות. בממד אחד עוסקים בהעתק, מהירות ותאוצה לאורך ציר, ומשתמשים בארבע משוואות הקינמטיקה לתאוצה קבועה.

## Skill atoms

- Displacement vs distance; velocity vs speed
- Four constant-acceleration kinematic equations
- Sign conventions and free-fall near Earth's surface
- Selecting the appropriate kinematic equation for unknowns
- Stopping-distance and time-free problems (v² = v₀² + 2aΔx)
- Multi-stage motion (projectile from height, two-phase trajectories)
- Relative velocity and meeting/catch-up problems (1D)
- Interpreting x-t, v-t, and a-t graphs
- Average vs instantaneous velocity under constant acceleration
- Integrating v(t) and a(t) to recover position (calculus link)

## Level scope

- **physics1:** Constant-acceleration 1D kinematics at university rigor — multi-step problems, sign discipline, and equation selection. Calculus-based derivations of v(t) and x(t) are fair game; free-fall and relative-motion word problems are core.
