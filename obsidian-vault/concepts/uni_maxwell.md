# Maxwell's Equations

**HE:** משוואות מקסוול

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Maxwell's Equations
**HE:** משוואות מקסוול

Maxwell's four equations are the foundation of all of classical electromagnetism — and, with the Lorentz force law, describe every electromagnetic phenomenon we encounter.

> ארבע משוואות מקסוול הן הבסיס לכל האלקטרומגנטיות הקלאסית. הן מנבאות גלי EM, מאחדות חשמל ומגנטיות.

## Skill atoms

- Gauss's law for electricity and magnetism (no magnetic monopoles)
- Faraday's law and Ampère–Maxwell law with displacement current
- Integral vs differential form correspondence
- Consistency of wave solution from coupled E and B fields
- Symmetry between changing E and B fields

## Level scope

- **physics1:** Maxwell's equations as capstone E&M — identify each law, displacement current role, and how EM waves emerge.
