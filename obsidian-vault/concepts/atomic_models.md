## Skill atoms

- Thomson model (raisin pudding)
- Rutherford model (nuclear atom, scattering experiment)
- Bohr model: quantized orbits
- Energy levels: Eₙ = -13.6/n² eV
- Photon emission and absorption: ΔE = hf
- Spectral series: Lyman, Balmer, Paschen
- Limitations of Bohr model

## Level scope

- **hs_physics:** Bohr model and hydrogen spectrum — energy levels E_n = −13.6/n² eV and ΔE = hf for emission/absorption lines. Bagrut questions identify spectral series (especially Balmer) and compute photon wavelength or energy from level transitions.
