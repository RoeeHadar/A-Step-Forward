## Skill atoms

- Magnetic field: B (Tesla)
- Magnetic field of a long wire: B = μ₀I/(2πr)
- Force on a current-carrying wire: F = BIL·sinθ
- Force on moving charge: F = qv×B (Lorentz force)
- Right-hand rule
- Magnetic field inside solenoid: B = μ₀nI
- Circular motion of charge in magnetic field

## Level scope

- **hs_physics:** Magnetic force on wires and moving charges (F = BIL, F = qvB), right-hand rule, and circular motion in B fields. Electricity items often combine Lorentz force with uniform E and B fields or solenoid field formulas.
