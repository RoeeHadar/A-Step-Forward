# Newton's Laws of Motion

**HE:** חוקי ניוטון

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Newton's Three Laws of Motion
**HE:** שלושת חוקי ניוטון

Newton's three laws relate force, mass, and acceleration. Free body diagrams (FBDs) are the essential tool for applying Newton's 2nd law to any system.

> שלושת חוקי ניוטון מקשרים בין כוח, מסה ותאוצה. דיאגרמות גוף חופשי (FBD) הן הכלי המהותי ליישום החוק השני.

## Skill atoms

- Newton's 1st law: inertia and equilibrium
- Newton's 2nd law: ΣF = ma
- Newton's 3rd law: action-reaction pairs
- Free body diagrams
- Weight vs. mass: W = mg
- Normal force
- Tension in strings and cables
- Atwood machine
- Connected bodies (system of masses)
- Inclined planes (with and without friction)

## Level scope

- **hs_physics:** Central to the mechanics questionnaire; free-body diagrams and ΣF = ma appear in nearly every exam. Typical multi-part problems cover Atwood machines, inclines, tension, and connected masses with explicit FBD sketches.
