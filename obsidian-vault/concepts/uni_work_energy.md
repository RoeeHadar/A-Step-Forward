## Skill atoms

- Work definition W = Fd cos θ and vector dot-product form
- Kinetic energy KE = ½mv²
- Gravitational PE and reference-level choice
- Spring elastic PE U = ½kx²
- Work-energy theorem (ΔKE = W_net)
- Conservation of mechanical energy (with and without friction)
- Power P = Fv and rate of energy transfer
- Perpendicular force does zero work
- Non-conservative work and energy dissipation to heat
- Choosing energy vs force methods for a given scenario

## Level scope

- **physics1:** Work, KE/PE, work-energy theorem, and mechanical-energy conservation at university depth — including friction losses and spring systems. Energy methods should be preferred when path-dependent forces are absent; dot-product work is assumed.
