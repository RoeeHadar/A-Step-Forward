# Gravitation

**HE:** כבידה

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Gravitation — Newton's Law, Orbits, and Kepler
**HE:** גרביטציה — חוק ניוטון, מסלולים וקפלר

Newton unified the falling apple and orbiting moon under a single law: gravity is a universal force inversely proportional to the square of the distance.

> ניוטון איחד תפוח נופל ולוויין מקיף תחת חוק אחד: כוח גרביטציה אוניברסלי שתלוי במסות ובמרחק בריבוע.

## Skill atoms

- Newton's law of gravitation: F = Gm₁m₂/r²
- Gravitational constant G
- Gravitational field strength g = GM/r²
- Escape velocity
- Satellite orbital velocity and period
- Kepler's 3rd law: T² ∝ r³
- Gravitational potential energy

## Level scope

- **hs_physics:** Appears in mechanics as satellite/orbit and escape-velocity problems using F = Gm₁m₂/r² and Kepler's third law. Less frequent than circular motion on a string, but a full multi-part gravitation question is common in mock exams.
