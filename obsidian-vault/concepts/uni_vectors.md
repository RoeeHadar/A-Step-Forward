## Skill atoms

- Scalar vs vector quantities in physical contexts
- Vector component decomposition in 2D and 3D
- Magnitude from components (Pythagorean / 3D length)
- Unit vectors and direction angles with quadrant correction
- Vector addition and subtraction (component-wise)
- Dot product: magnitude, angle, and perpendicularity test
- Cross product via determinant and right-hand rule
- Choosing dot vs cross for physics (work, torque, magnetic force)
- Resolving forces and velocities into x-y (and z) components
- 3D angle between vectors via dot product and arccos

## Level scope

- **physics1:** Full 2D/3D vector algebra with dot and cross products, unit-vector normalization, and physics applications (F = qv × B, W = F·d). Expect component-first problem solving at university exam depth.
