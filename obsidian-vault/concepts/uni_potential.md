## Skill atoms

- Potential difference V = −∫ E·dl
- Potential of point charge V = kq/r
- Equipotential surfaces perpendicular to field lines
- Potential energy U = qV for charge in field
- Work to assemble charge configuration
- Relation E = −∇V (field as negative gradient of potential)

- **physics1:** Scalar potential methods for electrostatics; equipotentials and energy bookkeeping replace repeated vector superposition when possible.
