# Applications of Derivatives

**HE:** יישומי נגזרות

## Lesson overview

**Lesson:** Applications of Derivatives
**HE:** יישומי נגזרות

The derivative is the key to finding maxima, minima, inflection points, and solving optimization problems. Uses $f'$ and $f''$ to analyze function shape.

> הנגזרת היא המפתח למציאת מקסימום, מינימום, נקודות פיתול ובעיות אופטימיזציה. משתמשים ב-$f'$ וב-$f''$ לניתוח צורת הפונקציה.

## Skill atoms

- Critical points: f'(x)=0 or f' undefined
- First derivative test for local max/min (sign change of f')
- Second derivative test for local extrema (f'' sign at critical point)
- Concavity and inflection points (f'' sign change required)
- Full function investigation: domain, critical points, asymptotes, sketch
- Vertical, horizontal, and oblique asymptotes
- Optimization: set up Q(x) from constraint, solve Q'(x)=0, check endpoints
- Absolute extrema on closed interval [a,b]: compare critical values and endpoints
- Tangent line and linear approximation at a point
- Interpret f, f', f'' graphs and their relationships

## Level scope

- **calc1:** Major calc-1 application block; full function investigation and constrained optimization word problems are staple midterm/final questions.
