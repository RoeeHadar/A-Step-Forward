## Skill atoms

- Force on moving charge F = qvB sinθ
- Right-hand rule for force direction
- Force on current-carrying wire F = BIL sinθ
- Circular motion of charge in uniform B field
- Radius r = mv/(qB)
- Lorentz force with E and B combined
